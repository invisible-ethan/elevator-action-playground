Privacy Policy

This page explains what information the Elevator Action fan remake uses and why. The game does not ask for an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or other contact details.

Stored in your browser

The game saves your high score and whether sound is muted in your browser's local storage, so they are remembered next time you visit. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Advertising

This site shows ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads, to limit how often you see the same ad and to measure how ads perform. Depending on your choices, these ads may be personalised based on your visits to this and other websites.

Visitors in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and can change that choice at any time. You can also manage personalised advertising in Google's Ad Settings or at www.aboutads.info.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services for more detail.

Hosting

Like any website, the server that hosts this game may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of your visit, to keep the site running and secure.
